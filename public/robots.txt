# iTravelForHealth

> Medical tourism platform connecting international patients with trusted healthcare providers, hospitals, and treatment planning services.

iTravelForHealth helps patients travel internationally for affordable, high-quality medical treatment by providing treatment guidance, hospital matching, travel assistance, and patient support throughout the healthcare journey.
